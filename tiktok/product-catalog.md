## Pricing summary

- **Entry tier (≤ $12)**: incense varieties — easy first purchase, low friction

- **Higher tier ($30+)**: pyramids, premium orgone necklaces

Lead TikTok cold-traffic content with entry-tier items. Reserve mid/high tier for warm-audience or remarketing pieces.

## How to link

**Primary method — TikTok Shop product tag.** You operate the Brand Account on TikTok Shop directly. In the post composer, tap "Add product" → TikTok Shop → search for the SKU. The product card appears on the video; viewers tap and check out in-app. The SKU IDs in the table above (e.g. `inc-5`, `pyr-2`) correspond to the TikTok Shop product feed Mike maintains. See [workflow/seller-account-setup.md](workflow/seller-account-setup.md) for first-time access.

**Fallback method — bio link** (use only for untagged content like brand-mood posts):

- Bio link to a product: `https://3rdeyesupply.com/products/black-love-incense`
- Bio link to category: `https://3rdeyesupply.com/category/incense`

**Never link to** `hopehamster.github.io/3rd-eye-supply/...` from TikTok. That's the transitional URL — it works but it looks unprofessional and may break if DNS migrates.

**When to bio-link vs Shop-tag:** Shop-tag any video that features ONE specific product or features 2-3 closely-related products (e.g., a pair of incense). Bio-link for category overviews, brand atmosphere, education content without one specific SKU, or LIVE preview / hype videos that point to an upcoming LIVE.

## What to do when a product is out of stock

The site doesn't currently show stock state on product pages (Mike's working on it). If a customer DMs you saying "I ordered and it's been 3 weeks":
1. Forward the order email and customer info to Mike.
2. Don't promise a fulfillment date you don't know.
3. In your videos, don't feature products you suspect are low stock without checking with Mike first.
