# Voice & Claims — What You Can and Can't Say

The single most important policy file in this workspace. Read it once now, refer back when drafting any script.

Three rule-sets stack on you whenever you post:
1. **TikTok Community Guidelines + Commerce Policy** — TikTok pulls videos, suppresses accounts, or bans entirely.
2. **FTC Endorsement Guides** — the U.S. Federal Trade Commission can fine for undisclosed paid promotion or false health claims.
3. **3rd Eye Supply brand voice** — the in-house standards that keep the audience trusting you.

When the three conflict, the strictest wins. In practice they rarely conflict; they overlap.

## The NEVER list (do not say in any video, caption, or comment)

### Health / medical claims
- ❌ "Cures [anything]"
- ❌ "Heals [a body part / a condition]"
- ❌ "Treats [anxiety / depression / insomnia / migraines / chronic pain / ANYTHING medical]"
- ❌ "Prevents [illness / disease]"
- ❌ "Reduces inflammation / lowers blood pressure / detoxifies the body"
- ❌ "Better than [medication name]"
- ❌ Anything that compares the product to therapy, medication, or medical treatment

### Outcome guarantees
- ❌ "This WILL bring you [love / money / a soulmate / a job / abundance]"
- ❌ "Manifest your [specific outcome]" (the word "manifest" is itself shadow-suppressed on TikTok; the broader concept of outcome-promise is the legal issue)
- ❌ "Guaranteed to work"
- ❌ "Try it and watch what happens"
- ❌ "I got [outcome] in [timeframe] after I started using this"

### Pseudoscience presented as science
- ❌ "Blocks EMF radiation" (even on the Protection Orgone Necklace — yes it says that in the product description, but the product description is itself going to need a rewrite, and you SHOULD NOT amplify that claim on TikTok)
- ❌ "Scientifically proven to..."
- ❌ "Studies show that crystals..."
- ❌ "Vibrational frequency of [number] Hz aligns with..."
- ❌ "Quantum [anything]"
- ❌ Any specific number/measurement of "energy" or "frequency"

### Cultural mis-attribution
- ❌ Claiming Native American or Indigenous tradition without sourcing the specific tribe + practice
- ❌ "Ancient Egyptian secret" without sourcing
- ❌ Using "sacred" or "ceremony" with vague hand-waving
- ❌ Co-opting language from a tradition you don't have lineage or sourcing for

### Manufactured urgency / scarcity
- ❌ "Only X left!" (when there are more)
- ❌ "Selling out FAST!" (without it actually being true)
- ❌ "Last chance!"
- ❌ "Price goes up tomorrow!" (unless it actually does)

### Crypto / financial outcome claims
- ❌ "This crystal made me money"
- ❌ "Manifested $X" (combines two banned things)
- ❌ Any income-promise pattern, even framed as personal anecdote

## The ALWAYS list (use these reframes)

When you want to talk about benefits, here's the legal/policy-safe version:

| Banned phrasing | Replace with |
|---|---|
| "Cures anxiety" | "I keep this on my nightstand for nights when I have trouble settling." |
| "Heals you" | "Traditionally associated with [practice/use]." |
| "Manifests abundance" | "Used in [tradition] as a focal object during practice." |
| "Will bring you love" | "Traditionally burned at the start of an intentional moment with a partner." |
| "Blocks EMF" | "Made with copper spirals and crystals — a tradition that goes back to Wilhelm Reich." (Note: you do NOT have to defend Reich's theory; you can name it as a tradition without endorsing the science.) |
| "Magic" | "Ritual." or "Practice." |
| "Energy" (as a noun, when you mean "vibe") | "Atmosphere." or "Mood." |

## Disclosure (FTC)

You MUST disclose any "material connection" between you and 3rd Eye Supply. Material connections include:

- You're an affiliate / earning commission per sale
- You were given free product
- You were paid a flat fee
- You're related to or close friends with the owner (yes, this counts)
- You have any financial interest in the business

How to disclose, in order of clarity:

1. **Burn-in caption on the video itself:** "#ad" or "Paid partnership" or "Affiliate". Visible the whole time.
2. **First line of TikTok caption:** Start with `#ad` or `#sponsored`.
3. **In-app TikTok "Branded Content" toggle:** flip it ON.
4. **Verbally in the video** (gold standard but not required if 1-3 done): "Quick disclosure — I'm an affiliate of 3rd Eye Supply, link in bio."

You need at LEAST 1 and 2 for every promotional post. If TikTok ever investigates an account, they look for both.

## What about "I just like this product" content?

If you genuinely bought a product yourself, paid your own money, and got NOTHING in return (no commission, no free product, no relationship), you don't need to disclose. BUT:

- The moment any of those change (Mike sends you product, you start earning commission), the BACKWARDS content can still be flagged in an audit because you have an ongoing relationship.
- Safest practice: once you have ANY commercial relationship with 3rd Eye Supply, every video that mentions a 3rd Eye product gets `#ad`. Even old ones. Either disclose retroactively or take down.

## TikTok Shop specifics

If you're using TikTok Shop:

- The product link in your video automatically functions as a "paid promotion" signal — TikTok auto-applies some disclosure. Don't rely on it being enough. Add `#ad` anyway.
- The TikTok Shop policy for "Spirituality and metaphysical products" is its own category — some products are restricted in some regions. Confirm with Mike whether your products are all live in TikTok Shop or only some.
- Returns: TikTok Shop has its own return policy on top of 3rd Eye Supply's. Be honest about it.

## The "what if I think it's real?" question

Some of you may genuinely believe in the spiritual claims. The audience may be more skeptical. Don't fight that. Use the reframes. They work BECAUSE they're honest:

- The incense really does smell good and create atmosphere.
- The crystal really is a beautiful object that creates a focal point for meditation.
- The orgone necklace really is a piece of craft involving copper, resin, and stones.
- The ritual is real — whether you believe the metaphysics or not.

You don't have to claim the metaphysics on TikTok. The product survives on its physical / aesthetic / ritual merit. The audience that buys it brings their own meaning.

## Audit your own draft (do this every time)

Before posting, run this checklist:

1. ✅ No health claim words: cure, heal, treat, prevent, reduce, lower, detox?
2. ✅ No outcome guarantee: manifest, will bring, guaranteed, results?
3. ✅ No pseudoscience framing: blocks EMF, scientifically proven, X Hz frequency?
4. ✅ Cultural attribution sourced or absent (not vaguely waved at)?
5. ✅ No false urgency or scarcity?
6. ✅ Disclosure visible (caption + first-line hashtag + in-app toggle)?
7. ✅ Caption first line ≤ 80 chars and includes disclosure?

If any answer is "uncertain," rewrite. The cost of a banned account is months of rebuilding.

## When you're not sure — use `/tiktok-audit`

If you have Claude Code, the `/tiktok-audit` command runs this checklist programmatically against your draft. It catches things human review misses.

You can also ask Mike. Better to ask before posting than to delete after a flag.
