# TikTok Policy Guardrails for Spiritual / Metaphysical Sellers

Summary of TikTok's Community Guidelines + Commerce Policies as they apply to this niche. Not a substitute for reading TikTok's own docs (they change frequently) — but a working summary that covers the patterns that have actually gotten accounts pulled.

**Always check:** https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/ and https://seller.tiktok.com/university/policy-center

## The big categories TikTok enforces against

### 1. "Misleading information" — health / wellness adjacency

Spiritual products live in a gray zone. TikTok's misinformation policy explicitly targets:
- Medical claims about products without scientific backing
- "Alternative medicine" that positions itself as a substitute for medical care
- Claims about products' effects on physical or mental health

**What this means for our content:**
- Don't position any product as a treatment for a mental health condition (anxiety, depression, ADHD, etc.).
- Don't position any product as a substitute for therapy or medication.
- Don't make specific physiological claims ("lowers cortisol", "balances hormones", "reduces inflammation").
- DO position products as ritual objects, sensory experiences, or cultural traditions.

### 2. "Commercial content" without disclosure

TikTok's Branded Content Policy requires the in-app toggle for any paid promotion. Failure mode: shadow-suppression of the video, eventually account-level restrictions.

**What this means:**
- Always flip the "Branded Content" toggle in the post composer for affiliate / commission / sponsored content.
- The toggle is in addition to `#ad` in your caption — not a replacement.
- Even "soft" content (lifestyle videos that include the product) needs the toggle if you have a commercial relationship.

### 3. Cultural appropriation / harmful content

TikTok's hate speech and offensive content policies apply more strictly to spiritual content than most creators expect.

**What this means:**
- Don't claim authority over a closed tradition (Native American, Indigenous, specific lineages of Buddhism, etc.) unless you genuinely belong to it.
- Smudging with white sage is a frequent ban-vector — many videos featuring it get pulled if framed as "Native American practice" without sourcing. (Note: this is also why our catalog leans on palo santo / dragon's blood / generic incense — fewer cultural-attribution risks.)
- Don't satirize, parody, or "make light of" spiritual practices, even your own. The audience reads it as disrespectful.

### 4. Spam / low-quality commercial signals

TikTok actively suppresses accounts it reads as low-effort commercial:
- Posting the same product 5+ times in a week
- Captions that are just hashtags
- Hard CTAs ("BUY NOW", "LIMITED TIME", multiple exclamation points)
- Using `#fyp` `#viral` `#foryou` on every post
- Reposting identical content
- Stitched videos that don't add genuine value

**What this means:**
- Vary your hooks even when the product is the same.
- Write actual captions, not just hashtag dumps.
- Use soft CTAs.
- 4-7 hashtags max, mix niche + broad.
- Repurpose content carefully — change at least the hook + caption, ideally also the edit.

### 5. Banned product categories (intermittently enforced)

Some product categories rotate in and out of TikTok's restricted list:

| Category | Status (2026-05 estimate) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Crystals / general | ✅ Allowed | Most allowed, watch claims |
| Incense | ✅ Allowed | Watch sage-cultural-attribution |
| Tarot decks | ⚠️ Allowed but watched | Don't position as "future-telling" |
| Orgone | ⚠️ Allowed, watch EMF claims | Avoid health / radiation positioning |
| Spell kits / curse-related | ❌ Often restricted | Avoid entirely in our catalog (none in 3rd Eye Supply) |
| "Manifestation" courses | ❌ Restricted | Not in our catalog |
| Psychic readings / live divination | ⚠️ Allowed with caveats | Not in our catalog |

### 6. Music / sound copyright

TikTok has a Commercial Music Library specifically for business / commercial content. Using a viral sound that's NOT in the CML on a `#ad` video will:
- Mute the audio when it exceeds personal-use thresholds
- Sometimes pull the video entirely
- Always limit reach

**What this means:**
- For any `#ad` post, use sounds from the Commercial Music Library.
- "Trending sounds" on TikTok are often NOT in the CML — check before adding.
- You can still use trending sounds for personal/non-commercial content, but those videos shouldn't link to the bio for sales.

## Account-level signals TikTok watches

If your account is reading as "low quality commercial" overall, all your videos suffer. Watch:

- **Profile photo / bio:** clear, descriptive, not just product spam. Bio link should go to a clean landing page, not an Amazon listing.
- **Following / follower ratio:** following many, followers few = bot-like. Don't mass-follow.
- **Video posting frequency:** 1-3/day is the sustainable rhythm; 8/day looks like a content farm.
- **Comment activity:** answer comments. Accounts that never engage drop in priority.

## What happens when you violate

TikTok's enforcement is graduated:

1. **First "borderline" content** → video gets reduced reach. You won't be notified.
2. **First clear violation** → video removed, you get a strike notification. Account remains.
3. **Repeated violations** → account-level limits (live disabled, monetization disabled, posting limits).
4. **Severe or repeated** → permanent ban. Can't appeal once enforced.

**If you get a strike**, don't immediately repost similar content. Take 7 days off, post something obviously safe, then resume. Reposting the flagged content gets your account flagged permanently.

## What to do if a video gets pulled

1. Don't panic-delete other videos — that signals guilt and can trigger account-level review.
2. Read the strike reason carefully. TikTok tells you which guideline was violated.
3. Match it against this doc + [voice-and-claims.md](voice-and-claims.md) — was the claim out of bounds?
4. Appeal ONLY if you genuinely think it was a mistake (e.g., the audio was actually from CML). Don't appeal claims you actually made.
5. Inform Mike — he tracks compliance across the whole brand.
