# TikTok Shop Compliance — Seller Side

3rd Eye Supply is an approved TikTok Shop seller. You operate the Shop directly (no outside affiliates). That means: every video, every LIVE, every product tag — TikTok treats as official brand content. Compliance failures hit the BRAND'S Shop, not just one video.

Two policy layers stack on every post:
1. **TikTok Community Guidelines** — applies to all content
2. **TikTok Shop Seller Policy** — additional, stricter rules for seller-tagged content

When the layers conflict, the stricter wins. In practice they overlap.

Always check current rules: https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/policy-center

## What happens when a Shop video is flagged

This is the strike chain that matters:

1. **First borderline content** → reduced reach. No notification.
2. **First clear violation** → video removed. Strike notification appears in Seller Center (NOT regular TikTok).
3. **Repeated violations on same product** → that product gets restricted in TikTok's search/recommend, and may be removed from the Shop catalog automatically.
4. **Pattern of violations on the seller account** → "Seller Health Score" drops. Below threshold = lose access to LIVE, lose access to Shop product tagging, eventually full Shop suspension.
5. **Severe / repeated** → permanent Shop closure. Can take 6+ months to appeal back; sometimes not possible.

Mike sees ALL of this from his side as the account owner. Every strike notification, every score change, every catalog restriction. Coordinate fast if you ever see one.

## Shop-specific never-do list (on top of [voice-and-claims.md](voice-and-claims.md))

These are restrictions that apply to TikTok Shop content specifically — many would be allowed on personal TikTok but are banned for tagged-product content.

### Restricted product categories for tagging

TikTok Shop's "Spirituality" subcategory has its own internal rule set. Most of our catalog is tag-able, but some may be conditional:

| Category | Tag-able status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Incense (non-cultural) | ✅ Yes | Avoid claiming health effects |
| Crystals / pyramids | ✅ Yes | No "manifests money/love" claims |
| Orgone jewelry | ⚠️ Conditional | EMF claims are auto-restricted — don't expand on the Protection Orgone description's EMF mention |
| Tarot decks | ✅ Yes | Don't position as "future-telling" |
| Rings | ✅ Yes | Standard jewelry rules apply |
| White sage / "smudge" products | ❌ Often restricted | Cultural appropriation flag — none in our catalog currently |
| "Spell kits" / "curse-removal" | ❌ Always restricted | None in our catalog |
| Astrology / divination services | ❌ Restricted as services | Services aren't tag-able, only physical goods |

If a product you tag later gets restricted: you'll get a strike AND the product becomes un-tag-able. Confirm tag-ability in Seller Center → Products before featuring any product in a planned LIVE.

### Content rules that go BEYOND regular TikTok

1. **No "free product" claims.** "Tag a friend and I'll send you a free incense" — instant Shop flag. You can run giveaways but they have to be registered through Seller Center as promotions, not improvised in a video.

2. **No pricing inconsistency.** If your video or LIVE says "$10 incense" but TikTok Shop has it at $12 (or vice versa), Shop flags the video for misleading pricing. Always use the live Shop price. During LIVE: cross-check the price on the product card before you call it out verbally.

3. **No comparison claims about other sellers.** "Better than [other brand]'s crystal" gets pulled. Shop's content policy treats comparative claims about other Shop sellers as restricted.

4. **No before/after claims with the product as the cause.** "Before [crystal] / After [crystal]" framing is banned for tagged products. Even on personal TikTok this is risky; on Shop it's an automatic strike.

5. **No fake reviews / fake testimonials.** TikTok Shop has a separate Review Manipulation policy. Don't ask friends to post "I bought this and it worked!" comments. Shop's bot detection catches it and the BRAND gets flagged.

6. **No misleading demonstrations.** If you light incense, don't edit smoke to look heavier than it is. If you show a crystal "catching light," don't add an effect that wasn't there. Especially in LIVE — improvised demos must be 100% real.

7. **No outcome guarantees during LIVE improvisation.** This is the most common Shop-strike vector for spiritual sellers — saying things in unscripted LIVE that you wouldn't write in a scripted post. Practice the policy-safe reframes (see [voice-and-claims.md](voice-and-claims.md)) until they're automatic.

## Disclosure — simplified for owner-operator

Because you ARE the brand (not an arms-length affiliate), the disclosure situation is much simpler:

- ✅ **Permanent bio disclosure on the Brand Account:** "Official TikTok of 3rd Eye Supply" or "Co-owner of 3rd Eye Supply" — this is your standing disclosure. FTC accepts persistent, obvious brand-owner status as adequate.
- ✅ **TikTok Shop product tags** automatically display a "Sponsored" / "Promoted" badge. That's TikTok's signaling layer.
- ❌ **You do NOT need `#ad` on every post.** That's for affiliates and arms-length endorsers. Brand-owner-operated accounts skip per-post disclosure.
- ❌ **You do NOT need the Branded Content toggle for your own brand's posts.** That toggle is for "this post is sponsored by ANOTHER brand" — not applicable to your own.

The exception: if you ever post a video that endorses a NON-3rd Eye Supply product (a partner brand, a friend's product), THAT post needs `#ad` and the Branded Content toggle. For your own brand's products from your Brand Account: bio disclosure is enough.

See [ftc-disclosure-rules.md](ftc-disclosure-rules.md) for the federal layer.

## Seller Health Score — the meta-metric

In Seller Center → Account → Compliance → Seller Score, you'll see a numeric rating (out of 5, typically). It's the aggregate of:

- **Late fulfillment rate** (Mike's side — keep <2%)
- **Order cancellation rate** (combined)
- **Return / refund rate** (combined)
- **Customer service response time** (your side — <24h is target)
- **Policy violation count** (content side — your responsibility)
- **Listing quality** (Mike's side — accurate descriptions)

A drop in seller score affects:
- Product visibility in TikTok search/recommend
- LIVE selling eligibility
- Access to promotional tools
- TikTok's willingness to surface your account to new audiences

It takes 30-60 days to recover from a meaningful drop. Don't take risks that compound.

## LIVE-specific compliance

LIVE selling adds its own rules layer on top of everything above:

- **No exaggerated demos.** During LIVE, you can't claim things you can't substantiate in real-time. "This incense has been burning for 20 minutes and the room is clean" — only say it if it's true and you can show it.
- **No undisclosed scripting.** Affiliate LIVE sessions can be planned but viewers shouldn't be told something is happening spontaneously when it's scripted.
- **No flash sales without coordination.** If you say "use code XYZ for 20% off in the next 10 minutes," that code has to actually exist in the Shop and apply to the products you're showing.
- **Pin authentic comments only.** Don't pin a fake "I just bought one!" from a friend. Pin the real buyer comments.
- **Don't go LIVE under the influence.** TikTok has specific "intoxicated content" enforcement on LIVE.

See [/workflow/live-selling.md](../workflow/live-selling.md) for the full LIVE playbook.

## What gets your Shop suspended

The cliff-edge events:

1. **Three policy strikes in 30 days** → temporary product-tag suspension
2. **Pattern of false-claim flags** → product category restrictions
3. **Manipulation patterns** (fake reviews, bot followers, fake testimonials) → permanent Shop suspension
4. **Selling restricted goods** → permanent
5. **Severe trademark / IP infringement** → permanent + potential legal action

None of these are theoretical. Spiritual sellers are in TikTok's highest-attention compliance bucket. Operate accordingly.

## When TikTok Shop pulls a video

1. The video disappears from TikTok.
2. You and Mike both get strike notifications in TikTok Seller Center.
3. The product card from your video stops driving sales.
4. If it's a clear violation, that product may become un-tag-able for 7-30 days.

What to do:
- Don't repost similar content for 48-72 hours.
- Read the violation reason carefully. Cross-check against [voice-and-claims.md](voice-and-claims.md).
- Tell Mike immediately — he may want to file an appeal from the brand side (more effective than your appeal alone).
- Don't argue in public comments about the takedown. Customers will ask; reply privately or not at all.
