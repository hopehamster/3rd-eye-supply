# TikTok Video Structures — 3rd Eye Supply

Five structures that work for spiritual/metaphysical products. Pick the one that fits the product + the hook category from [hook-templates.md](hook-templates.md).

## Structure 1 — Symptom → Reframe → Product (30-45s)

The PASTOR skeleton compressed for TikTok. Source: Ray Edwards, *How to Write Copy That Sells*.

```
0-3s:   Hook (Symptom-aware hook from hook-templates.md)
3-10s:  Amplify — name the second annoyance the symptom causes
        ("...and the worst part isn't the smell, it's that you stop
        wanting to be home")
10-18s: Story / reframe — "what shifted for me when I started [ritual]"
        Show the product in passing, don't pitch yet
18-25s: Solution — name the product, show it being used, ONE sentence
        about what it actually is
25-30s: CTA — "Link in bio if this lands. The whole catalog's at the
        same price point so you don't have to commit before you try"
```

Best for: incense, lower-ticket items, cold traffic.

## Structure 2 — Tradition Explainer (45-60s)

The "guide not hero" structure (Donald Miller, *Business Made Simple*). You're not selling — you're teaching the tradition, and the product comes in as the natural conclusion.

```
0-3s:   Tradition / explainer hook
3-15s:  Origin — where the material/practice comes from, who used it
        traditionally, what it was for. Visual: B-roll, not your face.
15-30s: Misuse vs use — what people get wrong (this is where you
        differentiate from the generic spiritual TikTok content)
30-45s: The product, briefly. "If this is the thing you've been
        looking for, here's where I get mine." Show the actual product.
45-55s: Honest caveat — "It won't [outcome claim]. What it does is
        [grounded sensory experience]. That's the whole point."
55-60s: CTA — soft. "Link in bio. Or just remember the tradition; you
        don't have to buy anything to start."
```

Best for: pyramids ($50-65), orgone ($24-40 premium), tarot deck.

Why the soft CTA at the end matters: this audience smells hard-sell from miles away. Saying "you don't have to buy anything to start" RAISES conversion, not lowers it, because it shows you're not desperate.

## Structure 3 — POV Day-in-the-Life (15-30s)

The shortest format. Inhabits a moment. The product is in the frame but not the point.

```
0-2s:   Visual: specific scene (kitchen at 7am, desk before client call,
        bedroom on Sunday)
2-15s:  Show the moment unfolding. Light the incense, wear the ring,
        place the pyramid. NO voiceover, or one casual sentence.
15-25s: A title card or single line appears: "[Product name] — $[price]
        — link in bio"
25-30s: End on the visual (smoke, candle, sunlight on stone), not on
        you talking.
```

Best for: rings, daily-wear orgone, incense as ambient. NOT good for higher-ticket — the audience needs more justification at $40+.

## Structure 4 — Comment-Response (15-25s)

When a previous video got engagement, mine the comments for the next 3 videos.

```
0-3s:   "I keep getting this comment on the [previous product] video"
        — screenshot of the comment fills the frame
3-15s:  Answer it honestly. If the comment is "does this actually
        work?" — you say "it's not 'working' in the way you mean.
        Here's what it does..."
15-20s: Show the product briefly
20-25s: CTA — refer them to the original post + bio link
```

Best for: any product that's already gotten a winning video. This is how you turn one hit into 4-5 follow-up hits. The algorithm rewards keeping a topic alive.

## Structure 5 — Unboxing / Tear-down (45-60s)

Honest review aesthetic. Works especially well for orgone (because people are skeptical) and crystal sets.

```
0-3s:   Hook: "Here's what's actually in a $30 orgone necklace"
        (or "$60 amethyst pyramid", etc.)
3-15s:  Open it. Show the materials. Hold it up to light.
15-35s: Walk through each component — copper, resin, the specific
        crystal. Name the materials honestly. "It's resin. It's
        not magic. The materials are real."
35-50s: Why someone would still want this — the craft, the aesthetic,
        the tradition. Not the energy. The reason a person who isn't
        gullible would still buy this.
50-60s: Price + link. Plain. "$30, $40 for the premium. Link in bio."
```

Best for: orgone, crystal sets, anything where the audience suspects "this is fake."

Why it works: respects the audience's skepticism, doesn't fight it. Convinces by *not* trying to convince.

## General Pacing Rules

- **Cuts every 1.5-3 seconds.** Static shots over 4 seconds lose retention unless they're inherently cinematic (smoke, candle flame, light through crystal).
- **No more than 15 words of voiceover in any 5-second window.** The platform is sound-first, captions-second; if you talk too fast OR too slow you fall off the rhythm.
- **End on the product, not your face.** The last frame is what people see when they're deciding to like/save.

## Captions On Always

Every video needs captions burned in (CapCut auto-captions, then edited). Reasons:
1. 80% of TikTok plays are sound-off in the first 1-2 seconds.
2. The algorithm reads on-screen text for topic classification.
3. Accessibility — actual people use closed captions.

Edit auto-captions to remove "um", "like", and add punctuation. Auto-generated captions are 70% there; you have to finish the job.

## Length Targets

| Structure | Target length | Acceptable range |
|---|---|---|
| Symptom→Reframe→Product | 35s | 25-50s |
| Tradition Explainer | 55s | 45-75s |
| POV | 20s | 12-30s |
| Comment-Response | 20s | 15-30s |
| Unboxing | 55s | 40-75s |

TikTok's algorithm in 2026 still favors 15-60s for cold traffic. Over 60s only when the structure justifies it (Explainer, Unboxing). Anything <12s tends to feel rushed unless it's a pure POV moment.

## What NOT to Film

- Tutorials on "how to manifest with this crystal." (Outcome claim — banned.)
- "Day in my life as a witch" type content branded with specific products. (Performative; alienates the buyer.)
- Ironic/comedy takes on spiritual practice. (You're selling to the people who take it seriously.)
- Anything where you ask the audience to "do this with me at midnight on the full moon for X to happen." (Outcome guarantee — banned.)
