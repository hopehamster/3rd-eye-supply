# TikTok Caption Formulas — 3rd Eye Supply

The caption is where the algorithm reads your topic and where customers decide if they tap. As the brand-owner-operated account, your FTC disclosure is permanent in the bio (see [/policies/ftc-disclosure-rules.md](../policies/ftc-disclosure-rules.md)) — so captions get cleaner than they'd be for an affiliate.

## The 3-Line Frame (use for almost every post)

```
Line 1:  Hook restated or extended — same energy as the video opening
Line 2:  Context / story / claim (within policy bounds)
Line 3:  Soft CTA + hashtags
```

Example (Symptom-aware hook):

```
The apartment feels stale every Sunday and you don't know why.

It's not the candles. It's that you don't have a transition cue —
just one consistent thing that marks "this is my time now."

Black love incense — tap the product if this lands.
#incensetok #ritual #cozyhome #spiritualtok
```

Note: no `#ad`, no `#TikTokShopAffiliate`. Your bio disclosure does the FTC work; you're the brand, not an affiliate.

## CTAs That Work

Soft CTAs convert better than hard CTAs for this audience. Spiritual buyers smell desperation.

**Primary CTA — TikTok Shop product tag:**

When you've tagged a product in the video (via "Add product" → TikTok Shop in the post composer), the product card IS the CTA. Reference it casually in the caption:

✅ Soft CTAs that reference the product tag:
- "Tap the product if this lands."
- "Linked in the post — $10, the cheapest way to try the catalog."
- "Tap the card if you want one. You don't have to buy anything to start — the tradition's free."
- "If you saved this, the [product] is tagged below."

**Fallback CTA — bio link or "shop":**

Some content shouldn't be product-tagged (brand-mood posts, category overviews, LIVE-promo posts, education without a specific SKU). For those:

✅ Acceptable for untagged content:
- "Shop link in bio."
- "Catalog's at 3rdeyesupply.com"
- "Going LIVE [day] — come hang out"

❌ Hard CTAs (avoid in all formats):
- "BUY NOW!"
- "LIMITED TIME OFFER!"
- "Don't miss out!"
- "Click the link before it's gone!"
- "Tag a friend who NEEDS this!"

The hard CTAs work for impulse-buy commodities. Spiritual products are considered purchases — even at $10. The audience is buying intentionally. Match their energy.

## When you need to mention LIVE selling

For posts that announce or recap a LIVE:

✅ Pre-LIVE announcement:
> "Going LIVE [day] [time] — walking through the new pyramid arrivals + a couple incense restocks. Drop a 🌿 if you want me to demo anything specific."

✅ Post-LIVE recap:
> "Highlights from last night's LIVE. [Product] sold out, restocking next week. Next LIVE is [day] [time]."

Don't post during the actual LIVE — TikTok auto-promotes the LIVE to your followers. Your "going LIVE" notification handles it.

## Disclosure (simplified for owner-operator)

You don't need disclosure in most captions. Your bio carries it permanently. The exceptions:

| Scenario | What to add |
|---|---|
| Brand Account, 3rd Eye Supply product, tagged | Nothing in caption (bio + product tag = enough) |
| Brand Account, 3rd Eye Supply product, untagged brand-mood post | Nothing (bio is enough) |
| Brand Account, featuring ANOTHER brand's product | `#ad` first line + Branded Content toggle |
| Personal account, mentioning 3rd Eye Supply | Bio note + `#ad` |

For 95%+ of your captions: skip disclosure tags entirely. Cleaner caption = stronger hook.

## The Caption Quality Test

Before posting, check:

1. **First line ≤ 80 characters?** TikTok truncates after ~80 chars with "...more". The first line is what gets seen.
2. **One claim, one ask?** Don't try to push three products in one caption.
3. **No banned phrases?** Quick scan against [/policies/voice-and-claims.md](../policies/voice-and-claims.md).
4. **Hashtags at the end, not woven through?** Hashtags in the middle of sentences look spammy and lower trust.
5. **Soft CTA referencing the product tag?** Match the actual mechanic.

## Hashtag placement

Always at the END of the caption, separated from prose by a line break. 4-6 hashtags is the sweet spot — fewer and you miss discovery, more and the algorithm reads it as spammy.

Specific bundle recommendations: [hashtag-bundles.md](hashtag-bundles.md).

## Emoji discipline

Use sparingly. One or two max. Spiritual TikTok vocabulary:

- 🌙 — generic spiritual / lunar / feminine practice
- ✨ — magic / wonder (sparingly — overused)
- 🪬 — protection (Hamsa hand) — culturally specific, use only when relevant
- 🕯️ — ritual / candle / incense
- 🔮 — divination
- 🌿 — herbalism / earth / palo santo

❌ Avoid: 💸 💰 (money emojis — looks scammy in this niche), 🤑, 🚨 (urgency).

## Captions for each video structure

### Symptom→Reframe→Product

```
[Specific symptom restated]
[Reframe in one sentence]
[Product name — tap the card to grab one]
#[niche] #[mid-tier] #[broad]
```

### Tradition Explainer

```
What [material/practice] actually is, in 2 sentences.
[Honest line about what it doesn't do]
[Product name — tap below if it lands]
#spiritualtok #[material]tok #[specific-tradition]
```

### POV

```
[One-line scene description matching the video]
[Product name tagged below]
#pov #[product-category]
```

### Comment-Response

```
Replying to @[user]'s comment — here's the version that's true.
[2-sentence answer]
[Original product — tap below]
#replying #[product-category]
```

### Unboxing / Tear-down

```
What's actually in a $[price] [product type]:
[Materials list in 1-2 lines]
[Honest line: "It's not magic. It's [craft] + [tradition]. Worth it
if you want [specific use case]."]
[Product tagged]
#unboxing #[product-category]
```

### LIVE announcement

```
Going LIVE [day] [time].
Walking through [categories] + [special promotion if applicable].
[Soft hook: "Drop a 🌿 if there's anything specific you want me to demo"]
#3rdeyesupply #incensetok #livestream
```

## When to NOT post

If the caption requires you to:

- Promise an outcome ("this WILL bring you...")
- Make a health/medical claim ("cures", "heals", "treats")
- Manufacture urgency that isn't real ("ONLY 3 LEFT" when there are 47)
- Mention a price that doesn't match the live Shop price

...don't post. Reshoot or skip the product. One Shop-flagged video can suppress the catalog for weeks.
