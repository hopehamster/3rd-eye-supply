# Weekly Rhythm — LIVE-Centered

You're running the TikTok Shop for 3rd Eye Supply. The week orbits around 2 LIVE selling sessions. Regular video posts feed the algorithm between LIVES. Friday is reflection. Sunday is off.

## The week shape

| Day | Time | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 90 min | **Batch-filming day.** Shoot 8-12 regular videos for the week's queue (see [batching-system.md](batching-system.md)). |
| **Tue** | 60 min edit + 90 min LIVE | **Edit Monday footage** in the AM. **LIVE 8pm** (90 min — see [live-selling.md](live-selling.md)). |
| **Wed** | 45 min | Post 1-2 of Monday's videos with product tags. 30 min engagement on other creators' videos. |
| **Thu** | 45 min | Post 1-2 more videos. 30 min replying to comments on your own posts + checking Seller Center customer messages. |
| **Fri** | 45 min | Post 1 video. 30 min reading the week's analytics (see [analytics-review.md](analytics-review.md)). Send Mike the weekly recap. |
| **Sat** | 90 min LIVE | **LIVE 11am** (90 min). Recap-and-post a highlight clip in the evening. |
| **Sun** | — | Off. |

Total: ~10 hours/week, 4-6 regular posts + 2 LIVES. Sustainable indefinitely.

## The daily check-in pattern

Whatever you do that day, start with a 5-minute open:

1. Open TikTok Seller Center → Home → check sales overnight + any alerts
2. Open Customer Service tab → quick scan of any new messages
3. Check Mike's text/DM thread → any operational notes
4. Then start the day's focused block

## Tuesday — the LIVE-day rhythm

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 10am | Open Seller Center, confirm yesterday's order status. Text Mike: "LIVE tonight at 8 — fulfillment good?" |
| 11am-12pm | Edit Monday's footage into 8-12 videos. Save to queue. |
| 2pm | Pick LIVE product lineup (8-12 SKUs). Stack sticky notes. |
| 3pm | Set up LIVE-specific promotions in Seller Center (flash discount, LIVE-only price). |
| 4pm | Tech check — phone, tripod, lighting, mic, charge, wifi. |
| 5pm | Eat. Hydrate. Walk. Don't work right up to LIVE — it shows on camera. |
| 7:30pm | Final setup. Pull up Seller Center → LIVE control panel on laptop (secondary screen). |
| 8:00pm | Go LIVE. |
| 9:30pm | Wrap. Send Mike post-LIVE update. |
| 10pm | Done for the day. Don't edit, don't analyze, just be done. |

## Saturday — the same shape, different time

Saturday 11am LIVE works because it captures Saturday-morning meditation/journaling audience for spiritual goods. Setup mirrors Tuesday but compressed:

- 9am: open Seller Center, message Mike, confirm lineup
- 10am: tech check, set up
- 11am: GO
- 12:30pm: wrap + post-LIVE recap to Mike
- 3pm or 4pm: post a quick highlights clip from the LIVE

## Wednesday/Thursday/Friday — the post-and-engage rhythm

These days are short blocks. Don't let them stretch.

### Wednesday block (45 min)

- **0-5 min:** daily check-in (above)
- **5-20 min:** post 1-2 videos from queue. For each:
  - Confirm caption + hashtags (use [caption-formulas.md](../content-system/caption-formulas.md))
  - Tag the product via "Add product" → TikTok Shop in the post composer
  - Confirm bio disclosure is current ("Co-owner of 3rd Eye Supply" or similar — see [/policies/ftc-disclosure-rules.md](../policies/ftc-disclosure-rules.md))
  - Post
- **20-45 min:** engagement — spend 25 min in comments of OTHER creators' spiritual/wellness videos. Leave 2-sentence comments that respond to the actual content. This earns you reach more reliably than hashtags.

### Thursday block (45 min)

- **0-5 min:** daily check-in
- **5-20 min:** post 1-2 more videos
- **20-35 min:** reply to comments on your last 3 days of posts. Reply by name. Be substantive.
- **35-45 min:** competitor research — open 3-5 spiritual TikTok accounts, screenshot 2-3 hooks you find interesting, note them in a `tiktok/research-notes.md` file for inspiration on the next Monday batch.

### Friday block (45 min)

- **0-5 min:** daily check-in
- **5-15 min:** post 1 video (the strongest of the queue's remaining)
- **15-40 min:** analytics review (see [analytics-review.md](analytics-review.md))
- **40-45 min:** send Mike the weekly recap:
  > Week of [date]: posted [X] videos, ran [Y] LIVES, ~[$Z] GMV. Top-selling [product]. Lowest performer [product]. Next week I'm planning [thing]. Anything to know on your side?

## What NEVER to do daily

❌ **Post more than 2 videos in a day** (excluding LIVE highlight clips). Algorithm reads as spam.

❌ **Delete an underperforming video in the first 6 hours.** Takes 12-48h for TikTok to find the audience.

❌ **Check Seller Center obsessively.** Once in the morning, once in the evening, plus during LIVE. That's it.

❌ **Edit + post the same day on a non-LIVE day.** The 24-hour gap between filming and posting catches mistakes.

❌ **Run a LIVE without telling Mike beforehand.** Even an impromptu "I'm just going LIVE for 30 min" can spike orders he can't fulfill.

## When you have a hit (>50K views in 24h)

A hit changes the day. Three actions:

1. **Reply to every comment in the first 12 hours.** TikTok weights creator engagement on rising videos.
2. **Pin the strongest social-proof comment** ("I just bought one", "exactly what I needed", etc.).
3. **Don't post anything else that day or the next.** Let the hit breathe.

Then on day 3-4: post a Comment-Response video (see [/content-system/video-structures.md](../content-system/video-structures.md)) referencing the hit. Algorithm extends the run.

## When you have a hit during a LIVE (>2K concurrent viewers)

Different rules apply:
- Slow down. Don't rush products to capture attention; let the audience breathe.
- Stay 2-3× longer than planned on the products that are converting.
- Have Mike on standby — you may pull 100+ orders in one LIVE.
- After the LIVE: send Mike the URGENT recap so fulfillment can absorb the spike.

## The first 4 weeks look different

Week 1: skip LIVES. Post 5-7 regular videos. Get comfortable with caption + tagging mechanics.

Week 2: skip Tuesday LIVE, run Saturday LIVE only. 60 min, fewer products (5-6). Learn the rhythm.

Week 3-4: full schedule. 2 LIVES + 4-6 regular posts.

Don't try to do everything week 1. The 48h fulfillment SLA + your own learning curve compound; build slowly.

## When you miss a day

Don't try to make it up by posting 4 videos the next day. Resume the rhythm. The audience would rather see consistent quality than panic catch-up.

## Sunday off — actually off

The single most common burnout pattern for spiritual content creators is treating it as 24/7 work. The audience can tell when content is grinding-fueled vs. when it has space around it.

Take Sunday off. Don't check Seller Center. Don't peek at comments. The brand survives one quiet day per week.
