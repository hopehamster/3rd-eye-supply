# Batching System — Film 8-12 Videos in 90 Minutes

The single biggest unlock for sustainable TikTok content. Most beginners spend an hour per video and burn out in a month. Batching = same setup, multiple products, multiple hooks, in one session. Marginal cost of video #8 = ~5 minutes.

## The night before — plan the shoot

**Time: 15 minutes**

Don't show up to film without a plan. Write out:

1. **5-7 products you'll feature** — pick a mix:
   - 2-3 incense (the workhorses)
   - 1-2 pyramid or higher-ticket
   - 1-2 jewelry or rings
   - 1 accessory or wildcard

2. **Per product: write 2-3 hooks** using [hook-templates.md](../content-system/hook-templates.md). You'll only film 1-2 per product but the extras give you options.

3. **Identify the "structure" per video** from [video-structures.md](../content-system/video-structures.md). Don't film an unboxing AND a tradition explainer in the same shot — they need different setups.

Output of this 15 min = a shoot list like:

```
1. Black love incense — Symptom hook ("apartment feels stale every Sunday") — Symptom→Reframe structure (35s)
2. Black love incense — POV hook ("light the first incense of fall") — POV structure (20s)
3. Amethyst pyramid — Tradition explainer hook — Tradition Explainer structure (55s)
4. Gaia incense — POV hook (after-screen-time wind down) — POV structure (20s)
5. Orgone Healing necklace — Unboxing hook ("what's actually in a $24 orgone necklace") — Unboxing structure (55s)
6. Crystal Statement Ring — POV hook (morning hand shot) — POV structure (20s)
7. Clear Quartz Pyramid — Tradition explainer hook — Tradition Explainer structure (45s)
8. Mayan Pyramid Incense — Symptom hook (no, source carefully) — Symptom→Reframe structure (35s)
```

## The shoot — 90 minutes

### Setup (15 min)

- **One filming spot.** Same lighting, same background, same camera angle. The variation comes from products and hooks, NOT from setup changes.
- **Recommended spot:** near a window with indirect natural light, a clean wood/stone surface (small table, mantel, desk), and 1-2 plants or texture-rich backdrop elements. Not against a white wall — too clinical.
- **Phone on a tripod, eye-level, vertical.** No handheld unless the video specifically calls for it (rare).
- **Lavalier mic if you have one.** Built-in phone mics work but lav is 3× better.
- **Bring all 5-7 products to the spot. Bring matches/lighter for incense. Bring a ceramic dish or stone slab for the smoke.**

### Hooks first (30 min)

Film just the 0-3s hooks for ALL the videos, one after another. Don't go through full videos yet.

Why: hooks are the highest-leverage part. If you film them while fresh and energized, they're stronger. If you film them after 90 minutes of being on camera, they're tired.

For each product, film 2-3 hook variations on the same camera setup. Total: 15-20 hook clips in 30 minutes. Pace yourself: 60-90 seconds per take, ~5 takes per hook to nail it.

### Bodies + visuals (30 min)

Now film the "body" of each video — the demo, the product close-ups, the smoke curling, the hand-modeling, the unboxing.

These don't have to be on-camera you. In fact, most should NOT be — switch to product-only B-roll shots:
- Smoke from incense in slow motion
- Pyramid on a window sill with morning light
- Ring on a hand, multiple angles
- Necklace lifted out of a small box
- Two products side-by-side for comparison

Get 30-60 seconds of B-roll per product. You'll cut it down dramatically in editing.

### CTAs + outros (10 min)

For each video, film the final "link in bio" callout if you're including one (not all videos should — see [video-structures.md](../content-system/video-structures.md) for which structures end on the product, not on you).

If you say the same CTA line 8 times, that's fine. Don't film 8 wildly different CTAs — pick ONE soft CTA and use it consistently across the batch. Builds brand voice.

### Wrap (5 min)

- Put products back where Mike's labeled them (don't lose track of inventory).
- Transfer footage to your laptop or cloud BEFORE you eat or take a break. Footage left only on phone is footage about to be lost.
- Make a quick text note of which clips were the strongest takes — your editor brain in 24h won't remember.

## Editing day — the next day

**Time: 60 minutes for 8-12 videos = 5-7 min/video**

For each video:
1. Pull the hook clip + body clips into CapCut / your editor.
2. Cut to length (15-60s depending on structure).
3. Add captions (auto-generate, then fix manually — see [caption-formulas.md](../content-system/caption-formulas.md)).
4. Add ONE simple transition between cuts. Not the fancy stuff. Plain cuts work better in this niche.
5. Add CML music if needed (see [policies/tiktok-policy-guardrails.md](../policies/tiktok-policy-guardrails.md) — Commercial Music Library only for `#ad` posts).
6. Export.
7. Write the caption + hashtags using [caption-formulas.md](../content-system/caption-formulas.md) and [hashtag-bundles.md](../content-system/hashtag-bundles.md).
8. Save to queue / drafts. **Don't post yet** — let it sit for 24h.

If a video is taking >10 minutes to edit, something's wrong: either the hook was weak or you're over-editing. Move on, come back later.

## The queue — pacing

After Tuesday editing, you have 8-12 videos in the queue. Don't post them all at once.

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Wed | 1-2 videos |
| Thu | 1-2 videos |
| Fri | 1 video |
| Sat | 1 video |
| Sun | off |
| Mon | (next batch shoot) |

That spreads 7-8 posts over the week — sustainable, varied, and gives the algorithm time to find an audience for each.

Keep 2-4 videos in reserve for emergencies (sick day, hit video that needs a follow-up, breaking trend you can ride).

## Tools

Minimum:
- Phone (any iPhone or Android from 2022+)
- Free CapCut

Nice to have:
- Tripod ($20)
- Lav mic ($25-50)
- Ring light ($30) — but natural light is better for this niche; ring light reads as "influencer fakery"

Don't buy:
- DSLR / mirrorless camera unless you already know how to use it. Phone footage performs better on TikTok.
- Drone shots, complex transitions, motion graphics. The audience reads them as over-produced; the algorithm reads them as ad-style.

## When batching breaks down

If you find yourself filming 1-2 videos at a time multiple days a week instead of one big batch:

- **Are you over-perfectionist?** Set a 5-take limit per hook. Move on.
- **Are you running out of products?** Check with Mike for new SKUs or rotate back to your top performers.
- **Are you bored of the setup?** Vary lighting (window AM vs PM), seasonal background changes, single new prop. Don't redesign the whole setup.

The discipline pays off in week 3. Stick with it through the boring middle.
