# LIVE Selling Playbook — TikTok Shop (Seller-Side)

LIVE selling is the centerpiece of the 3rd Eye Supply TikTok strategy. As the brand operator (not an outside affiliate), you have access to seller-side tools that make LIVES significantly more powerful than what affiliates can do — flash discount codes, free-shipping promotions, exclusive LIVE pricing, multi-product pinning.

Spiritual goods are uniquely well-suited to LIVE: visual, demoable, sensory, and the audience is conversational by nature. A well-run 2-hour LIVE can earn more than a week of regular posts.

## Why LIVE is the centerpiece for this catalog

- **Demo-friendly products.** Incense smoke, crystal light-catching, ring close-ups, pyramid weight — all read MUCH better in real time than in cut/edited video.
- **Conversation-driven category.** Spiritual TikTok audiences ask questions ("does it really clear energy?", "how do I use it?"). Recorded video can't answer those; LIVE can.
- **Bundle-friendly inventory.** Incense pairs well with crystal pyramids; jewelry pairs with rings. LIVE lets you build the case for a multi-product purchase in real time.
- **Trust building.** Watching someone use a product for 90 minutes is more convincing than 20s of cut video. Conversion rates on LIVE-tagged products typically run 3-8× regular videos.

## When you can go LIVE — eligibility

As the brand operating account, you have access from day one. There's no follower threshold for sellers (vs. ~1K for personal LIVES). But the algorithm still rewards stable accounts:

- ✅ Zero active community guideline strikes
- ✅ TikTok Shop compliance score is "healthy"
- ✅ Mike's fulfillment is ready for the order spike (always confirm 12-24h ahead)
- ✅ You've watched 2-3 other spiritual / wellness LIVES end-to-end
- ✅ Your filming setup is stable (lighting, audio, charged phone)

Recommendation: post 4-6 regular videos in the first week to establish your account voice, THEN add LIVE. Going LIVE on a brand-new dormant account can read as suspicious to the algorithm.

## Pre-LIVE prep (60 minutes, day before)

### 1. Coordinate with Mike (5 min)

Send the standard pre-LIVE message:
> Going LIVE [day] [time]. Featuring [top 3-5 products]. Fulfillment OK for ~[N] expected orders?

Wait for his confirmation BEFORE you announce the LIVE publicly. If he says hold off (he's traveling, low stock, etc.), reschedule.

### 2. Pick the product lineup (15 min)

8-12 products, ordered from cheapest to most expensive (commitment-ramping). Mix:
- 4-5 entry-tier ($8-15 incense — the audience-warmers)
- 2-3 mid-tier ($25-30 rings, basic orgone)
- 1-2 higher-ticket ($50+ pyramid, premium orgone)
- 1-2 wildcard (tarot, accessories)

Write each on a sticky note. Stack them in order. You'll work down the stack during the LIVE.

### 3. Set up LIVE-specific promotions (15 min)

This is the seller-side superpower. In TikTok Seller Center → Marketing → LIVE:

- **Flash sale**: pick 1-2 products to discount 10-15% during the LIVE only. Auto-applied at checkout for buyers who tap during the session.
- **Free shipping threshold**: e.g., free shipping over $30. Encourages bundling.
- **LIVE-only price**: badge appears on the product card during LIVE — creates legitimate urgency (it actually does end when the LIVE ends).

Don't go crazy with promotions. Two specials max per LIVE. More than that and it feels like a clearance event, not a curated experience.

### 4. Prep talking points per product (15 min)

For each of the 8-12 products, write 3-5 bullet points:
- What it is (1 sentence)
- Traditional use or sensory feel (no health claims — see [voice-and-claims.md](../policies/voice-and-claims.md))
- Materials / craft / origin
- Who it's for
- Why this one and not the catalog variant

Don't write a script. Bullets only. LIVE is conversational; reading sounds wooden.

### 5. Tech check (10 min)

- Phone on tripod, eye-level, vertical (TikTok LIVE is vertical-only)
- Lighting: window-side natural OR diffused soft light (no harsh ring light)
- Lavalier mic if you have one — audio quality is the #1 retention factor
- Phone fully charged (LIVES drain battery FAST; consider keeping it plugged in)
- Stable wifi or strong cellular — a dropped LIVE doesn't auto-resume
- Test the LIVE function in TikTok Seller Center beforehand

### 6. Announce the LIVE (5 min)

12-24h before going LIVE, post a normal TikTok:
> Going LIVE tonight at 8pm walking through the incense catalog and the new pyramid arrivals — drop a 🌿 if you want me to demo anything specific.

People who comment in advance get a notification when you go LIVE. This is the cheapest reach amplifier on the platform.

## During the LIVE — the rhythm

### Minutes 0-5 — Open warm

Don't pitch immediately. Open warm:
- Greet people by name as they enter ("Hey Sarah, good to see you")
- Sit casually, not staged
- Have something visual happening — light an incense as ambient, place a pyramid in the frame
- Mention what the next 90 minutes will look like ("Tonight I'm walking through the incense catalog and a couple of the pyramids — drop your questions and I'll demo what catches you")

**Why the first 5 minutes matter:** TikTok's LIVE algorithm watches early retention. A LIVE that holds >50% of viewers past minute 5 keeps getting new viewers pushed to it. A LIVE that drops below 30% stalls.

### Minutes 5-25 — First product cycle

Start with the cheapest product (typically an $8 incense). Walk through:
- Pick it up, show it
- Sensory: what it smells like, what tradition it's from
- Light it (smoke is cinematic on LIVE)
- Talk for 5-8 minutes while it burns
- Tap "Add product" in the LIVE control panel → confirm the product card is showing
- Mention the LIVE-only price if applicable
- "If you want one, tap the card at the bottom — link's there for the rest of the LIVE"

Keep moving. Don't dwell on a product after the demo lands.

### Minutes 25-90 — Middle stretch

Cycle through the rest of the products. 5-10 min each. Vary the pace:
- Demo product → 2 min Q&A from comments → next product → quick "what we've covered" recap → next product

Pin a comment near minute 30: "All products linked in the LIVE — tap the cards as we go." Latecomers can browse without you re-pitching.

When a product gets strong engagement (people commenting "I'm buying!", purchase notifications popping), STAY on it longer. Don't rush to the next. Ride the wave.

### Minutes 90-120 — Close

Last 30 minutes is when impulse buyers convert. Strategy:
- Recap the 2-3 highest-engagement products from earlier
- Re-pin the product cards
- Mention the LIVE-only price is ending: "Going to wrap at the top of the hour — if you've been thinking about [product], the LIVE price is gone after that"
- Don't manufacture fake urgency. The LIVE ending is real urgency; let that do the work.

### Closing the LIVE

- Thank by name the people who bought
- Mention the next LIVE date if you have one
- End on a calm note — not on a sales push
- Save the replay (TikTok Seller Center → Marketing → LIVE → Replays)

### Right after wrap

Send Mike the post-LIVE update:
> LIVE wrapped. Approximately [X] orders. Top sellers: [list]. Anything for fulfillment to know?

This lets him batch fulfillment efficiently and catch any orders that need clarification.

## The comment stream — your real job

Half your job during a LIVE is reading and engaging the comment stream. Common types:

| Comment | Response |
|---|---|
| "How does it smell?" | Honest sensory description; pick up the product and describe it as if to one person |
| "Does it really work?" | "It's not 'working' in the way you mean. Here's what it does — [grounded, no health claim]" |
| "I just bought!" | Thank by name; pin if it's a particularly enthusiastic message |
| Question you don't know | "Great question — I'd have to check with Mike on the exact source. Drop it in the comments and I'll DM you tomorrow" — DO NOT make it up |
| Skeptical / dismissive | Engage warmly, never defensively. "Yeah, totally fair — here's why someone skeptical might still find this useful..." |
| Trolling / disruptive | Pin a constructive comment; ignore the troll; have a moderator block if needed |

Have a moderator if possible — your spouse, a friend, anyone who can pin comments, filter trolls, and answer logistical questions while you focus on the demo.

## Seller-side LIVE tools you should use

Things affiliates can't do, but you can:

1. **Flash discount codes** — set up in advance via Seller Center, announce during the LIVE
2. **LIVE-only pricing** — appears as a badge on the product card during the LIVE
3. **Pin multiple products simultaneously** — show 2-3 related items together
4. **Free-shipping promotions** — encourage bundle purchases
5. **Custom thank-you message** — auto-sent to LIVE buyers
6. **Schedule the LIVE** in advance so it shows up in viewers' notifications
7. **Co-host with another creator** (Mike or a guest) — splits screen, doubles audience overlap

Use 2-3 of these per LIVE. Stacking all 7 is too much.

## Compliance during LIVE (in addition to [/policies/tiktok-shop-compliance.md](../policies/tiktok-shop-compliance.md))

Same never-do list applies — no health claims, no outcome guarantees, no pseudoscience. Live improvisation is the highest-risk format for accidentally saying something flagged. Practice the policy-safe reframes BEFORE going LIVE so they're automatic:

- "What does this do for me?" → "Traditionally used for [X]. People say [Y]. Honestly, what it does is create a small ritual moment in your space."
- "Will this manifest love for me?" → "I can't promise outcomes. What it does is create a focal point for a tradition that goes back centuries."
- "Does it block EMF?" → "It's made with copper and crystals — there's a tradition that goes back to Reich's work. I'm not going to claim it does anything measurable; I can tell you what it's made of."

When in doubt: describe the OBJECT and the TRADITION. Don't claim what the object DOES for the customer.

## LIVE cadence — sustainable rhythm

The fulfillment SLA is the constraint, not your energy. With Mike's fulfillment in play:

- **Tuesday 8pm** — 90 min LIVE
- **Saturday 11am** — 90 min LIVE

That's 2 LIVES/week, 180 min on camera, sustainable indefinitely. Mike's fulfillment has time between to clear the queue.

Avoid:
- Consecutive-day LIVES (overlapping audience cannibalizes)
- Same-day LIVE + regular post (splits attention)
- LIVES over 2 hours (energy drops, retention dies)
- LIVES under 60 minutes (algorithm reads as low-effort)

## Promoting the LIVE

12-24 hours before:
- Post a regular TikTok teaser (see Pre-LIVE step 6)
- Update bio temporarily: "🔴 LIVE [day] [time]"
- Send a thank-you DM to recent buyers asking them to come hang out
- Cross-post the announcement to Instagram Stories if you cross-post

## After the LIVE

Within 12 hours:
- Post a recap video: "Highlights from last night's LIVE — [most popular product] sold out, restocking next week" — drives intent for the next LIVE
- Thank by name (publicly OR via DM) the biggest buyers
- Save the replay
- Clip 2-3 highlights (15-30s each) for future regular videos — repurpose the best moments

## Analytics — what to track

In TikTok Seller Center → Analytics → LIVE:

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Peak concurrent viewers | grow 10-20% per month | Indicates algorithm trust |
| Average watch time | >5 min | Below 3 min = retention issue |
| Click-through rate on product tags | >3% | Below 1% = wrong product picks |
| Conversion rate (clicks → purchase) | >5% | Below 2% = price or copy issue |
| Total GMV (gross merchandise value) | growing weekly | The number that pays for everything |
| Returns / refunds | <5% of GMV | High returns = mismatch between LIVE pitch and actual product |

Log these weekly. The patterns drive what to feature in the next batch shoot.

## Common LIVE failure modes

❌ **Going LIVE without checking fulfillment with Mike.** Orders pile up; SLA broken; seller score drops.

❌ **Promising stock you don't have.** Mike maintains inventory; if you say "we have 50 of these," it had better be true.

❌ **Inventing a tradition or origin on the fly.** "This is an ancient Mayan ritual practice" — when you don't know that's true, don't say it. Cultural mis-attribution is a fast strike vector.

❌ **Drinking on camera.** Spiritual TikTok audience generally reads this as disrespectful.

❌ **Cutting the LIVE short because it's slow.** A 30-min LIVE teaches the algorithm you abandon. Ride it to at least 60 min even if engagement is low.

❌ **Repeating products from the previous LIVE without variation.** Viewers tune in for new stuff. Rotate the lineup.

❌ **Talking AT the camera instead of TO the people.** Imagine one person watching, not an audience. The LIVE feels intimate even at 500 viewers.
