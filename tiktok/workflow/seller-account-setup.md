# TikTok Shop Seller Account — Setup & Roles

3rd Eye Supply is an approved TikTok Shop seller. Mike holds the seller account; Mike's wife operates the content side from her own machine. This file covers the access split, what each of you can do, and how to coordinate the operating handoffs.

## Two accounts, one Shop

There are TWO TikTok accounts in play:

1. **The Brand Account** — connected to TikTok Shop, owned by Mike/702 AI Services. This is what TikTok Shop is tied to. Account name is the brand handle.
2. **The Operator Account (optional)** — a personal-feeling account run by Mike's wife that represents the brand and links back to the Shop. May or may not exist depending on the content strategy.

**Recommendation:** start by posting from the **Brand Account only**. Adding a personal-feeling operator account later (once volume justifies it) is fine — but two accounts split your audience early, and 3rd Eye Supply benefits from concentrating attention on one identity.

For this file, assume you (the operator) are logged into the Brand Account on your machine.

## Access setup — first time only

### Option A: Shared login (simplest)

Mike shares the Brand Account credentials with you. You log in on your phone. Done.

Pros: zero friction; you can post immediately.
Cons: if either of you logs in from a new device, TikTok may force a re-verify; you both share 2FA SMS code visibility.

### Option B: TikTok Shop sub-account user (cleaner for accounting)

In TikTok Seller Center → Account Settings → Sub-Account Management, Mike can invite you as a sub-account user with specific permissions. You get your own login but operate the same Shop.

Permissions Mike should grant you:
- ✅ View Orders
- ✅ Manage Products (so you can see the catalog and tag in posts)
- ✅ Manage LIVE sessions
- ✅ View Analytics
- ✅ Manage Promotions / Discount Codes
- ⚠️ Maybe NOT: financial settings, payout settings, fulfillment provider settings (Mike's side)

This keeps a clean audit trail of who did what and means losing your phone doesn't lock the brand out of the account.

**Pick one, do it on day 1, don't revisit.**

## What you'll see in TikTok Seller Center

Open https://seller-us.tiktok.com on the web. Bookmark it.

| Tab | What's there | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Today's sales, recent orders, alerts | Open every morning |
| **Orders** | Order list, fulfillment status, returns | Mike's primary; you may glance |
| **Products** | The 34 SKUs in the catalog, stock levels, prices | Before tagging in a new video |
| **Promotions** | Discount codes, flash sales, free shipping | Setting up LIVE-only deals |
| **Marketing → LIVE** | Schedule a LIVE, see past LIVE replays + analytics | Pre-LIVE setup |
| **Marketing → Affiliates** | If/when we open to outside affiliates (not now) | Skip for now |
| **Analytics** | Sales, traffic sources, conversion by SKU | Friday review |
| **Customer Service** | Buyer messages | Check daily during posting cadence |
| **Account → Compliance** | Strikes, policy notifications | If a video gets flagged |

## What you control vs. what Mike controls

**You (operator):**
- TikTok content — videos, LIVE sessions, captions
- Product tagging in videos
- Setting up LIVE-only flash sales and discount codes (within parameters Mike sets)
- Replying to comments and DMs
- Customer messages routine inquiries ("when does it ship?", "is this in stock?")

**Mike (owner / fulfillment):**
- Product feed — adding/removing SKUs, prices, stock levels
- Order fulfillment — packaging and shipping orders (or coordinating with CJ for dropshipped items)
- Refunds and disputes
- Tax / payout / bank account settings
- Brand-level account compliance (responding to TikTok strikes)
- Major promotions ("20% off everything" type campaigns)

**Both, with coordination:**
- LIVE selling schedule — Mike needs to know when LIVES are happening so fulfillment can absorb the order spike. Don't surprise him.
- Inventory-sensitive content — if you're about to feature a low-stock product, confirm with Mike first.
- Negative reviews / public complaints — surface to Mike fast; he may want to handle the public response.
- Product copy / description on the WEBSITE — only Mike edits; if you spot a typo, DM him.

## The 48-hour fulfillment SLA — critical

TikTok Shop requires orders to ship within 48 hours of purchase. Miss the SLA repeatedly and:
- Your seller score drops
- Products lose visibility in TikTok's search and recommendation
- At enough strikes, the entire Shop gets suspended

**What this means for content cadence:**
- Don't go LIVE when Mike can't fulfill the resulting orders within 48h (he's traveling, the warehouse is closed, holiday weekend approaching).
- ALWAYS check with Mike before a LIVE: "Going LIVE Saturday 11am — fulfillment OK?"
- During holiday peaks (November-December), coordinate cadence carefully — fulfillment is the bottleneck, not content production.

## Coordinating with Mike — communication patterns

Define BEFORE you start:

1. **Daily check-in channel.** Text, Slack, iMessage — pick one and use it consistently. Don't bury operating-critical info in email.
2. **The "before LIVE" message.** Always send this 12-24h before a planned LIVE:
   > Going LIVE [day] [time]. Featuring [top 3 products]. Fulfillment OK for ~[N] expected orders?
3. **The "after LIVE" message.** Right after wrap:
   > LIVE wrapped. Sold approximately [X] orders. Top sellers: [list]. Anything for fulfillment to know?
4. **The "this looks weird" message.** If a customer DM mentions a problem you can't immediately resolve, forward to Mike same-day.

## When you find a website bug

You'll notice things in product copy, broken images, weird prices. DO NOT edit the website. DM Mike with:
- The product slug
- A screenshot or quote of the issue
- Your suggested fix (if you have one)

Mike will fix it on the site side (or open a ticket to Claude Code to do so on his machine). The Github repo is his surface; yours is TikTok content.

## What to NEVER do

❌ **Run a promotion that overlaps with one Mike's running.** Two simultaneous "20% off" codes = chaos in margin math. Coordinate.

❌ **Pause LIVE selling mid-stream without notifying Mike.** If you have to abort a LIVE, message him: orders may still be processing.

❌ **Reply to a 1-star review publicly without coordinating.** The brand voice on customer service is Mike's call; your voice is for content discovery.

❌ **Schedule LIVES on consecutive days during a busy week.** TikTok's algorithm cools down repeat LIVES; Mike's fulfillment can't absorb back-to-back order spikes.

❌ **Buy followers / engagement for the Brand Account.** Permanent ban risk for the WHOLE Shop, not just one account.
