5. Customer Analysis - ADDED
∙ Target Market 
The primary audience includes individuals aged 18-45 interested in spirituality,  wellness, and new-age practices. They often seek personal healing, self-discovery, and  alternative lifestyles. Secondary markets include cultural practitioners and collectors  of unique metaphysical items.

Customers seek products that resonate with their personal beliefs and practices, along  with the authenticity and quality of offerings. They also look for trusted guidance in  spiritual practices and may be interested in consultations for astrology or healing. 
∙ Customer Preferences 
The audience is largely active on social media and prefers brands with a unique,  authentic, and inclusive presence. They value sustainable sourcing and transparency  in product origin.
