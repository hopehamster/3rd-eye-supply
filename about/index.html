About 3rd Eye Supply

3rd Eye Supply is a new age metaphysical shop for the indigo generation and beyond. We offer a variety of natural handmade artisan crafts, crystal healing, aromatherapy, biogeometry and more. Our transformational collection rejuvenates the energy in your home and aura to help prevent dis-ease from stagnating energy and EMF pollution in the environment.

Our mission is to blend traditional wisdom with modern practices—connecting clients with a rich network of practitioners from around the globe while celebrating inclusivity and authenticity. We aim to nurture mind, body, and soul through products and culturally rooted spiritual services such as astrology, tarot, and reiki.

By embracing sustainable sourcing and transparency, we strive to be a trusted destination for holistic wellness solutions and spiritual guidance.
